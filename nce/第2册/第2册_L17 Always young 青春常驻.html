主线时态：一般现在时　说习惯、事实或现在的状态；主语第三人称单数时动词加 -s。　绿框＝主线，红点＝穿插（背景／铺垫）。

导问Why doesn't Aunt Jennifer tell anyone how old she is?
1
My aunt Jennifer is an actress.

主干：My aunt Jennifer ＋ is ＋ an actress

2
She must be at least thirty-five years old.

主干：She ＋ must be ＋ at least thirty-fiv…　can/may/must/should 等后接动词原形，语气、能力、推测都由情态动词承担。

3
In spite of this, she often appears on the stage as a young girl.

主干：she ＋ appears　说习惯、事实或现在的状态；主语第三人称单数时动词加 -s。

4
Jennifer will have to take part in a new play soon.

主干：Jennifer ＋ will have ＋ to take part in a n…　will + 动词原形，说将来的动作或临时的判断。　宾补从句（不定式）：动词后接不定式作宾语补足语。

5
This time, she will be a girl of seventeen.

主干：she ＋ will be ＋ a girl of seventeen　will + 动词原形，说将来的动作或临时的判断。

6
In the play, she must appear in a bright red dress and long black stockings.

主干：she ＋ must appear　can/may/must/should 等后接动词原形，语气、能力、推测都由情态动词承担。

7
Last year in another play, she had to wear short socks and a bright, orange-coloured dress.

主干：she ＋ had ＋ to wear short socks…　说过去某时发生、已结束的动作，常与 last week / yesterday / in 1994 同用。　宾补从句（不定式）：动词后接不定式作宾语补足语。

8
If anyone ever asks her how old she is, she always answers, 'Darling, it must be terrible to be grown up!'

主干：she ＋ always answers　说习惯、事实或现在的状态；主语第三人称单数时动词加 -s。　条件状语从句（If）：提出条件，主将从现（主句将来时、从句现在时）。
