导问What is one of the features of modern camping where nationality is concerned?
1
Economy is one powerful motive for camping, since after the initial outlay upon equipment, or through hiring it, the total expense can be far less than the cost of hotels.

主干：Economy ＋ is ＋ one powerful motive…　时间状语从句（since）：说主句动作发生的时间，注意从句用现在时表将来。

主干：it ＋ is　定语 popular 修饰名词
